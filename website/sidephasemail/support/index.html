SidePhase Mail / support

Support & data removal

Effective / last updated: 29 October 2026.

Get in touch

Email support@sidephasestudio.com for account setup, app problems and privacy enquiries. Include your app version (Settings → About), Windows version, provider name, the steps you took and the error shown. Screenshots are useful after you hide private information. Never send your password, app password, OAuth tokens, a complete mailbox database or unrelated private messages.

Gmail during beta

Choose Add account → Google, enter your address and finish sign-in in your system browser. Select the requested Gmail access, then return to the app. The app tests incoming and outgoing connections before saving the account. Gmail sign-in currently requires an approved test account while Google public verification is prepared; customers will not need to create Google Cloud projects. In Google Testing mode, access can expire and require sign-in again.

If a sign-in expires

Use Settings → Accounts → Sign in again for the affected account. Check that you choose the matching Google address and allow the requested mail permission. Network outages can resolve without another sign-in; an expired or revoked grant needs your consent again. Workspace administrators may restrict third-party clients. Microsoft sign-in is still being validated for public release.

Remove an account and its data

  1. Open Settings → Accounts and remove the account. This removes its active cached mailbox and saved sign-in from that installation; it does not delete mail from the provider.
  2. Cancel or remove unwanted queued messages in the Scheduled/outbox view. Queued work is retained after account removal so it is not silently lost.
  3. For Google, visit your Google Account’s third-party connections and remove SidePhase Mail access if you also want to revoke the app’s Google grant. Removing a local account alone does not revoke access on your other devices.
  4. To remove every remaining app-held local file, use Settings → About → Data folder to locate the folder, close the app, then delete that folder. This also removes other accounts and preferences in that installation, so export anything you want to keep first.
  5. Delete exported attachments, device backups and any separate portable/installed copies you no longer need. Contacts, queued attachments, recovery files and older database backups can otherwise remain.

Clear local data only clears cached messages. Messages will download again while the account remains connected. To request removal of support correspondence you sent us, email support; we do not hold a central copy of your mailbox.

Where Windows keeps app data

The portable edition normally uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\SidePhase Mail. The installed edition uses its package’s LocalState folder under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages. Settings → About → Data folder shows the exact location for the copy you are running. Windows normally removes installed app data on uninstall; retained copies, exports and backups are separate.

Report an issue or follow development

The GitHub project contains source and build instructions. You can report a public issue, but use email for sensitive support details and redact both. See the privacy policy for data handling and the terms and licence notes for beta limitations.
