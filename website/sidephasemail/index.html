Private by design

Your mailbox.
Your computer.

Your mail connects directly to your provider. It does not pass through SidePhase Studio servers.

  • Google sign-in opens in your system browser. We never receive your Google password.
  • Account tokens are stored locally with Windows protection. Mail processing and search stay on your PC.
  • Remote images are blocked by default. Email scripts do not run.
  • Your Gmail data is never sold, used to target advertising, or sent to an AI service.

An honest beta

Being built.
Already connecting.

Google browser sign-in and Gmail inbox synchronisation have been tested with a real account. Gmail access is currently limited to approved test users while Google public verification is prepared. Microsoft sign-in is still being validated. Other supported IMAP/SMTP providers connect directly using their provider’s accepted sign-in method.

SidePhase Mail is not yet a production release. Back up important mail, and read the current project notes before trying a beta build. The project page contains source, build instructions and the latest development status.
